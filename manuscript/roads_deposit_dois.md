# ROADS deposit: items and DOIs

The data packages proposed in [`roads_deposition_candidates.md`](roads_deposition_candidates.md) are deposited in Flinders ROADS (figshare), in the private project **"genome_entropy: structural entropy of open reading frames"** (project 284049, created 2026-11-02).

> **Status (2026-11-20): 13 of the 14 items are published.** R1a-3 is uploaded but still a private draft until Figshare finishes processing its last four files. Each DOI below resolves to the latest version of its item, and version 1 can be cited as `<DOI>.v1`. The project itself is still private, which does not affect the items.

## Notes

- **Quota.** The account quota started at 250 GiB, of which 161.0 GB was already used, leaving 107.4 GB. That was not enough for R1a-3 and R1a-4 (70.9 GB together). The Library raised it to 350 GiB (375.8 GB), and after both items were uploaded 325.1 GB is used.
- **Upload check (2026-11-02).** 93.3 GB in 551 files across ten items. Every file is present. Figshare's computed MD5 equals the supplied MD5 for every file it has checked; eight R1a files were still in Figshare's internal checking queue at the time. The account now uses 254.3 of 268.4 GB.
- **Recheck (2026-11-05).** 543 of 551 files are `available` with matching MD5. The same eight R1a files are still stuck in Figshare's processing queue three days later: bac_008 and bac_009 (R1a-1) and bac_266 and bac_267 (R1a-2) are `moving_to_final` with matching MD5, and bac_011, bac_012, bac_270 and bac_271 are `ic_checking` with no MD5 computed yet.
- **Re-upload (2026-11-05).** The eight stuck files were deleted and uploaded again from the originals on Gadi, after each was checked against its manifest SHA-256 (`reupload_stuck.py` beside `deposit.py`). Figshare processed them within minutes. All 551 files in the ten uploaded items are now `available`, and Figshare's computed MD5 matches the supplied MD5 for every file. R1a-1 and R1a-2 are complete, with 202 files each.
- **R1a-3 and R1a-4 upload (2026-11-20).** `copyq` job 184668541 ran `stage R1a_3 R1a_4` and then `upload R1a_3 R1a_4`. All 364 files are present, with sizes matching the staged files and computed MD5 equal to supplied MD5. All 162 R1a-4 files are `available`. In R1a-3, 198 of 202 are `available`; bac_542, bac_543 and bac_544 are `moving_to_final` and bac_546 is `ic_checking`.
- **Publication.** R1a-1, R1a-2, R1b, R2, R3, R4a, R4b and R5a–c were published on 2026-11-19, and R1a-4 on 2026-11-20, each as version 1.
- **Every item contains** a `README.md` and a `SHA256SUMS.txt`. The checksums are the manifest values from [`roads/`](roads/), with paths as uploaded, or as they appear inside the archive. The uploader re-hashes every file from R1 and R4 against the manifest before sending it. The R5 files are checked with `sha256sum -c` after they are copied from Teams (`genome_entropy:General/LOGAN`). Archives are deterministic GNU tar streams compressed with zstd. Each archive extracts into a directory named after the archive.
- **Metadata.** Every item has:
  - **author:** Robert Edwards (ORCID 0000-0001-8383-8949). Adding co-authors to a published item creates a new version;
  - **group:** Flinders University;
  - **type:** dataset;
  - **categories:** Bioinformatic methods development; Genomics and transcriptomics; Microbial genetics;
  - **licence:** CC BY 4.0, the licence proposed in the candidates document. Change it before publishing if a different licence is chosen.
- **R4 (GMSC).** The GMSC authors agreed to redistribution on 2026-11-19 (open item 2 in the candidates document). Before publishing, a sentence was added to the R4a and R4b descriptions crediting the benchmark sets to the GMSC authors and asking users to cite Duan et al. (2024), *Nature Communications*, [doi:10.1038/s41467-024-51894-6](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-024-51894-6), which is also listed as a reference on both items. The GMSC group's smORF benchmark paper (accepted in principle) should be added to both items once it has a DOI.
- **Tooling.** The deposit was made with `/scratch/ob80/re3494/roads_deposit/deposit.py`, run as `copyq` jobs 182879460, 182879461 and 184668541. The item IDs and DOIs are in `state/`.
